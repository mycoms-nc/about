

# Privacy Policy for Alifbata

**Last Updated:** August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Alifbata** ("we," "our," or "us") handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through simple, transparent practices.

---

### 1. Account Creation and Login

Alifbata is designed for ease of use. **No login, account creation, or registration is required** to use the app. You can access all features without providing personal credentials, email addresses, or account details.

### 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We **do not collect, store, or share any personal user data**. However, to provide core functionality, the app accesses specific device features locally:

* **Location Data (Prayer Times):**
* **What we access:** With your permission, Alifbata accesses your device's approximate or precise location.
* **Why:** Location coordinates are sent to the third-party **Aladhan API** solely to calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times.
* **Data Handling:** Location data is used strictly in real-time for calculation purposes. We do not store, track, or share your location history on our own servers or with any advertising networks.



### 3. Local Network Sharing (Photo Transfer)

Alifbata allows you to share photos between your own Alifbata-enabled devices.

* **Local Network Transfer Only:** Photo transfers occur **entirely over your local Wi-Fi / local area network (LAN)**.
* **No Remote Servers:** Photos are sent directly from one local device to another. They are **never uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or shared with any third parties**.

### 4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party service to provide prayer times:

* **Aladhan API:** When requesting prayer time calculations, your location data is processed by the Aladhan API according to their service parameters. We encourage you to review the [Aladhan API Privacy Notice](https://aladhan.com) for details on their data processing.

### 5. Data Security and Sharing

* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics brokers.
* **On-Device & Local Processing:** Aside from fetching prayer times via the Aladhan API, all data processing (such as local photo transfers) stays strictly within your local environment and on your physical device.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Because Alifbata does not collect personal information, account profiles, or tracking identifiers, our application is safe for general audiences, including children.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates to the app or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our distribution pages with an updated "Last Updated" date.
